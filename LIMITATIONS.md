# Limitations & AI-Contribution Disclosure

- **Pool-restricted scope:** Every exact minimum applies **only** to subsets of the stated finite coordinate pool. Nothing here breaks a published record or settles the general minimal Kochen-Specker problem.
- **Kernel vs. certificate boundary:** Only `B(3,2) = 31` and `B(4,1) = 18` are proved inside Lean 4.35.0 (zero Mathlib, 9/9 `module` headers, zero warnings `--wfail`, checked by `lake env leanchecker --fresh Solution`, and accepted via `lake comparator --paranoid` by the official Lean kernel in default and paranoid mode and by lean4lean, nanoda, con-leche and con-ron). The other seven pools ($H=3,4$; roots of unity for $N = 6, 12, 18, 24$; dimension 5) rely on SAT/DRUP certificates checked by the project's own `verify/drup_check.c` outside Lean, on the CNF encoder and on paper lemmas. As of `v2.1.0`, `scripts/verify_lrat.sh` cross-verifies `B(3,2)`, `B(4,1)`, and `B(3,3)` in CI through pinned, SHA-256-verified upstream `drat-trim` → LRAT → `lrat-check` **and HOL4-formally-verified `cake_lpr`**; the other six pools have no third-party proof check, and their proof files are not kept in git. Full `cake_lpr` replay of all 83 dim-5 cubes remains on `ROADMAP.md`.
- **Lean tests and audit:** The three negative Lean checks in CI only confirm that `decide` fails on three false facts (pool sizes 48 and 39, a wrong bit count); they are sanity checks, not refutations of theorem variants. The 30-ray and 17-ray non-vacuity targets are one-line corollaries of the main theorems. Under Lean 4.35 `module` mode, the axiom audit covers 116 theorem constants in `KS`, 62 of them written by hand.

Its version 10 (23 November 2026), published after this repository's `v1.0.0` (10 November 2026), certifies these values and proves 31 for `B(3,2)` and 33 for `U(3,6)` in Lean.
