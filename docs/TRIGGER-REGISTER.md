observed and that would trigger the work. Nothing below is a claim of a result. The register of results

## How to use this file

* An item leaves this file only when the trigger has fired and the work is recorded in the register of
  results and the changelog.
* A new limitation found by a reader goes here with a trigger, not into a to-do list without one.
